ZS Player — Privacy Policy

App: ZS Player: Video Player & Cast (com.zemsk.videoplayer)
Developer: Zemsk Studio · Contact: zemskstudio@gmail.com
Effective date: 10 November 2026

ZS Player is a video player. It plays videos stored on your device or on your local network and can show them on a TV or projector. We do not require an account, we do not run our own servers, and we do not sell personal data. This policy explains what the app does with information and which third-party services it uses.

1. Information that stays on your device

  • Your videos. With your permission the app reads the list of videos on your device (Android media storage) to show your library, thumbnails and folders. Videos are never uploaded anywhere by us.
  • Playback data and settings. Playback positions, last watched video per folder, chosen audio/subtitle languages, theme, language, hidden folders and similar settings are stored only on your device.
  • Network folders (SMB). If you add a network folder, its address and the login you enter are stored only on your device and are sent only to that server on your network.
  • Hidden folder. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled entirely by Android. The app only receives "unlocked" or "not unlocked" and never has access to biometric data.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

2. Casting to a TV or projector

When you cast, the video is sent directly from your phone to the selected device on your local network (DLNA, Google Cast, Smart View / screen mirroring or an HDMI cable). To find devices the app sends standard discovery messages on your local network. During casting the phone runs a small local web server that is reachable only from your local network and only while casting. If the TV cannot play a file, the phone converts it on the device itself. Nothing is sent to us.

3. Wi-Fi transfer from a computer

If you open "Transfer from PC", the app runs a local web server on your network while that screen is open, so you can upload videos from a browser on the same network. Uploaded files are saved to your device. The server stops when you leave the screen.

4. Subtitle search (OpenSubtitles)

Only when you use "Find subtitles", the app sends the video's title or file name, a technical fingerprint of the file (the OpenSubtitles "movie hash", calculated from the file size and small parts of the file) and the languages you chose to OpenSubtitles.com, and downloads the subtitle you pick. If you enter an OpenSubtitles login, it is stored on your device and sent only to OpenSubtitles. Their handling of data is described in the OpenSubtitles privacy policy.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

After the free trial, the free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob (a banner in the library and, at most once every 25 minutes, a full-screen ad when you leave a video; never during playback). AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions, in order to show, measure and personalise ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice in the app's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. ZS Player Pro removes ads.

6. Purchases (Google Play)

ZS Player Pro is bought through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled by Google; we never receive your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own Pro.

7. Permissions

  • Videos / media — to show and play your videos.
  • Internet, Wi-Fi and network state, multicast — for casting, network folders, Wi-Fi transfer, subtitle search and ads.
  • Notifications and foreground service — to show playback controls while casting or playing in the background.
  • Biometric — optional, to unlock the hidden folder.

8. Children

ZS Player is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data security and retention

Data kept by the app stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data processed by Google (ads, purchases) and OpenSubtitles (subtitle search) is kept according to their own policies.

10. Your rights and contact

We do not hold personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to export or delete; you control the data on your device. For any question about privacy, write to zemskstudio@gmail.com.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
